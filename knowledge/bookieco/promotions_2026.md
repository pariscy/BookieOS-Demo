## Voucher / Free Bet — general rules
- Voucher is issued through the shop system when the conditions of the promotion providing it are satisfied and the ticket is finally settled.
- Customer presents/scans ticket at cashier; system provides a voucher code for use on self-service terminals.
- Voucher provides betting value, not cash refund.
- Each voucher can be used once.
- If voucher bet wins, winnings are paid normally and free-bet value is not deducted from winnings.
- If voucher bet is void under BookieCo general terms, voucher is reissued.
- May be used on BookieCo betting types subject to these rules.
- Cannot be used on singles; only multiples of 3+ selections.
- Must be used within the validity period specified by the promotion that issued it.
- Maximum voucher value is defined by each promotion.
- Maximum payout rules apply to voucher bets.
- Abuse/arbitrage intended to secure guaranteed gains can lead to voucher cancellation and reversal of related gains.
- Partial/full cash out: NOT available.
- Changes to voucher terms require Authority approval under Articles 23 and 24 of Advertising Code KD.02.2021; customers who already received a voucher remain bound by accepted terms unless changes are more favourable.
- BookieCo general terms and regulations apply.
